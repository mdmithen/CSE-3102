# 🏆 District-Based Sports Club Management System

### Centralizing Sports Excellence Across Bangladesh


The **District-Based Sports Club Management System** is a web-based platform designed to modernize and centralize sports club management across different districts of Bangladesh.


The system provides a unified digital platform for **administrators, club managers, players, and the general public**, making sports administration more transparent, efficient, and data-driven.


---


## 📌 Project Overview


Currently, many local sports clubs depend on:


- Paper-based records
- Manual player registration
- Disconnected spreadsheets
- Manual tournament scheduling
- Social media for announcements
- Fragmented player and club information


These approaches make it difficult to manage sports activities efficiently and provide reliable access to information.


Our proposed system brings these activities into **one centralized digital platform**.


---


## 🎯 Objectives


The main objectives of this project are:


- Digitize sports club management across districts.
- Maintain centralized player and club information.
- Simplify tournament and match management.
- Provide real-time rankings and points tables.
- Improve transparency in sports administration.
- Make player discovery and scouting easier.
- Reduce dependency on paper-based management.
- Provide the public with easy access to sports information.

## 👥 User Roles


The system is designed around multiple user roles:


| Role | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| 👨‍💼 Administrator | Manage districts, clubs, tournaments, users and system data |
| 🏟️ Club Manager | Manage club information, players, teams and matches |
| 🧑‍🎽 Player | Maintain profile and view personal performance information |
| 👥 Public User | View clubs, tournaments, rankings, news and player information |


---


## 🗺️ District-Based Management


The platform organizes sports clubs according to districts.


This allows users to:


- Browse clubs by district
- Find players by district
- View district-level tournaments
- Compare team rankings
- Discover talented players from different regions
