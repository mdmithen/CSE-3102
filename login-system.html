Get Started

🔐 Login

District Sports uses a single sign-in page for every role. Choose an account type when you sign in and you will be taken straight to the right dashboard.

  • 👨‍💼 Super Admin — manage clubs, districts and users
  • 🏟️ Club Admin / Club Manager — manage your club and its players
  • 🧑‍🎽 Player — view your profile, performance and rankings

→ Go to the Login Page

Don't have an account? Contact your Club Admin.
